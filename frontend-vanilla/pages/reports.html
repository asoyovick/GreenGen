Methodology and interpretation guidance

Statistical terms

  • Trend direction/magnitude: estimated change over time (e.g. Sen’s slope) with units.
  • Statistical significance: evidence against a no-trend hypothesis under stated assumptions; not proof of cause.
  • Confidence intervals: uncertainty range for estimates when provided.
  • Completeness: proportion of usable observations; missing data is never treated as zero.

Classification legend

  • Significant increasing trend
  • Significant decreasing trend
  • No significant trend detected (not “stable”)
  • Insufficient data
  • Inconclusive / unavailable

Observed trends do not by themselves establish causation. Always review dataset limitations, quality information, and provenance provided with results.
